# Aayam Office System - User Guide

This guide is designed for new users. It maps every step in the system from receiving an order to delivering the job.

---

## Phase 1: Marketing & Sales (Getting the Order)

**Goal:** Turn a phone call/inquiry into a confirmed Order.

1.  **Log a New Inquiry**
    *   Navigate: **Marketing > Leads**
    *   Action: Click **+ New Lead**
    *   Input: Client Name, Phone Number, Requirement.
    *   Result: Lead is saved.

2.  **Send a Quotation**
    *   Navigate: **Marketing > Quotations**
    *   Action: Click **+ Create Quote**
    *   Form: Select the Lead/Client. Add Items (e.g., "1000 Boxes"). Set Rate.
    *   Action: Click **Save**. Download PDF to send to client.

3.  **Confirm the Order**
    *   Navigate: **Marketing > Quotations** (Open the saved quote)
    *   Action: Click **Convert to Order** (Top right button).
    *   Result: This moves the data to the "Production" team.

---

## Phase 2: Production Setup (Admin/Manager)

**Goal:** Assign the work to the right person.

1.  **Create the Job**
    *   Navigate: **Sales & Finance > Orders**
    *   Action: Find the new Order. Click **Create Job**.
    *   Form:
        *   **Job Type**: Select "Box", "Label", "Visiting Card", etc.
        *   **Assign Design**: Choose a Designer OR select "Print Only" if file is ready.
        *   **Deadline**: Set the delivery date.
    *   Result: The job appears in the Designer's dashboard or Print Queue.

---

## Phase 3: Design Studio (Designer)

**Goal:** Prepare the file for printing.

1.  **Start Work**
    *   Navigate: **Design Studio > My Queue**
    *   Action: Find job in "To Do". Drag card to "In Progress".
    *   Action: Click **Upload Icon** to save draft files.

2.  **Quality Check (QC)**
    *   Action: Click the **Clipboard Icon** (Checklist) on the job card.
    *   Task: Check off "Bleed ok", "Colors CMYK", "Spelling Check".
    *   Action: Save.

3.  **Finish Design**
    *   Action: Drag card to **Ready for Print**.
    *   Result: Job moves to the Printer's list.

---

## Phase 4: Manufacturing (Print Operator)

**Goal:** Print and Finish the product.

1.  **Plan Printing**
    *   Navigate: **Projects & Jobs > Print Planner**
    *   Action: View all "Ready for Print" jobs.
    *   Action: Group jobs by Paper Type (e.g., "300gsm Art Card").

2.  **Update Status**
    *   Action: Change status to **Printing** -> **Finishing** (Cutting/Pasting).
    *   Action: When done, change status to **Ready for Dispatch**.

---

## Phase 5: Dispatch & Delivery

**Goal:** Check quality and send to client.

1.  **Final Quality Check**
    *   Navigate: **Dispatch**
    *   Action: Find the Job. Click **QC Checklist** icon.
    *   Task: physically count items, check print quality.
    *   Action: Mark as **Passed**.

2.  **Dispatch Item**
    *   Action: Click **Create Dispatch** (Truck icon).
    *   Input: Driver Name / Courier Service.
    *   Result: Generates Delivery Challan. Stock is reduced.

---

## Phase 6: Finance (Accounts)

**Goal:** Get paid.

1.  **Create Invoice**
    *   Navigate: **Sales & Finance > Invoices**
    *   Action: Click **+ Create Invoice**. Select the Order.
    *   Result: Official Bill is generated.

2.  **Record Payment**
    *   Navigate: **Sales & Finance > Payments**
    *   Action: Click **+ Record Payment**.
    *   Input: Amount received, Payment Mode (Cash/UPI/Bank).
    *   Result: Update "Outstanding Balance" for the client.
