Aayam Office System - Manual & Guide

This document explains how the Aayam Office System works, connecting every department from Marketing to Dispatch.

Simple Workflow:

1. LEAD → Client Enquiry
2. QUOTE → Price Estimate
3. JOB → Production Work
4. INVOICE → Payment

2. How Data Flows (The "Convert" System)

The system is designed so you never type data twice.

  • Lead → Client: Saves a temporary enquiry as a permanent client.
  • Client → Quote: Starts a price estimate for that client.
  • Quote → Job: Turns an approved estimate into a live Job (e.g. AAY-26-001).
  • Job → Invoice: Turns a finished job into a bill.

3. Production Steps

  1. Admin creates a Job and assigns it to a Designer.
  2. Designer sees it in "Design Tasks", works on it, and uploads proof.
  3. Client approves the design.
  4. Admin moves job status to "Printing".
  5. Printer Operator sees it in "CMYK Print", prints it.
  6. Dispatch Team sees it in "Dispatch", ships it.
  7. Accountant sees it "Completed", generates Invoice.
