## Step-by-Step Guide (After Deployment)

### Step 1: Add to Home Screen

1. **Open Safari** on your iPhone (NOT Chrome!)
2. **Go to:** `aayam-office-system.vercel.app`
3. **Login** with your credentials
4. **Tap the Share button** (the square with arrow pointing up) at the bottom
5. **Scroll down** and find "Add to Home Screen"
6. **Tap "Add to Home Screen"**
7. **Tap "Add"** (top right)

**Result:** An "Aayam" icon appears on your iPhone home screen (like any other app)

---

### Step 2: Open the App Icon

1. **Go to your iPhone home screen**
2. **Find the "Aayam" icon** (you just added it)
3. **Tap the icon** to open it

**Result:** The app opens fullscreen (no Safari bars!)

---

### Step 3: Allow Notifications

**What Happens:**
When you open the app for the FIRST time, a popup will appear asking:

```
"Aayam" Would Like to Send You Notifications
Notifications may include alerts, sounds, and icon badges.
```

**What You Do:**
Tap **"Allow"**

**Result:** Notifications are now enabled!

---

## If You Don't See the Popup

Sometimes iOS doesn't ask immediately. Here's how to enable manually:

1. Go to iPhone **Settings** app
2. Scroll down to **"Aayam"** (in your app list)
3. Tap **"Notifications"**
4. Turn ON **"Allow Notifications"**
5. Turn ON **"Lock Screen"**, **"Notification Center"**, **"Banners"**

---

## Testing Notifications

After enabling, test it:

1. Keep the app open
2. Go to Settings → Production
3. Look for any alerts or updates
4. You should see a notification!

**OR** ask someone to assign you a task - you'll get notified instantly!

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** "I don't see Add to Home Screen option"
- **Solution:** Make sure you're using Safari (not Chrome!)

**Problem:** "App doesn't ask for notification permission"
- **Solution:** Go to iPhone Settings → Aayam → Notifications → Enable manually

**Problem:** "Not getting notifications"
- **Solution:** 
  1. Check iPhone Settings → Aayam → Notifications are ON
  2. Check Focus mode isn't blocking notifications
  3. Try closing and reopening the app
